Zohar · Departure, a growing household and the time to return

How does the meaning of Jacob’s departure change as a household takes shape?

The departure from Beersheba is placed beside Ecclesiastes’ rising and setting sun. East, south, north and west give the journey a symbolic order alongside its geographical movement. Jacob does not cease to belong to a larger relation when he leaves home. At the same time, the night and the place at which he rests retain their particular narrative role. Begin by following the order of directions in the source, then ask what that order adds to the experience of departure.
