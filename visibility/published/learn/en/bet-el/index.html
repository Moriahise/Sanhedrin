Vayetzei · Waking at Bethel

What does Yaakov recognize when he wakes up?

Read Genesis 28:10–19 twice. On the first reading, follow what Yaakov does with the stone. On the second, mark each occurrence of “place.” In verses 16–17 he describes what he has just learned; in verses 18–19 he acts and gives the place a name.

Discuss together

  1. Which words in verse 16 describe the place, and which describe Yaakov’s earlier understanding?
  2. Compare the stone before the dream and after it. What does the second action add to his words?
