Zohar · Guarding the way and learning to bear the light

How do vigilance, gradual change and attachment to Torah belong together?

The heading about Jacob’s messengers leads to the angels charged with guarding a person’s ways. The opening then describes the evil inclination as a persistent accuser and seducer from the beginning of life. Protection is introduced beside the recognition of vulnerability. A person is not pictured as beyond struggle merely because a journey has begun. Read the named biblical verses and the account of the inclination together: the exposition supplies a setting in which vigilance and divine keeping can be discussed without confusing them.
