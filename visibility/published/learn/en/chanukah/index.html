Chanukah · Counting the lights

What are Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel counting?

Read Shabbat 21b from the passage beginning “Ner ish uveito.” Trace the two ways the Gemara explains the order of the lights. One explanation counts days still to come or days already entered. The other compares the decreasing offerings of Sukkot with the principle of ascending in holiness.

Read the sources

  • Talmud Bavli · Shabbat 21b

Discuss together

  1. Draw two rows for the eight nights. Which numbers do the two schools assign to the second night?
  2. Which explanation depends on the calendar, and which draws an analogy from another area of worship?
