Was the terrible fate of Chutzpis related to his moral failure in the story of Rabban Gamliel and R' Yehoshua?

On Berachos 27b, Rabban Gamliel continued to lecture while R' Yehoshua was in effect required to remain standing, as an expression of Rabban Gamli'el's disapproval. Some of those present objected to this treatment and told Chutzpis the announcer to stop.

On Chullin 142a, Acher saw the tongue of Chutzpis (which had been ripped out by the Romans) in a trash heap.

Someone suggested to me that tongue of Chutzpis was torn out and thrown in the trash to correspond to his moral failure in continuing to announce for Rabban Gamli'el until stopped by others. Is there any (other) source for this association between the two stories?
