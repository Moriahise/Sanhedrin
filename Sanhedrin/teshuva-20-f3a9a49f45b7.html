Question

Protection of schach from getting moldy

This year we have much rain in the NY-NJ area during Succot. This may cause danger going forward that bamboo mats used for schach would get moldy.

What can one recommend for protecting the schach from getting moldy? If the schach gets moldy, how can one clean it and kill the mold?

Answer

The only directly supported preventive recommendation is to store the bamboo mats completely dry and sealed/airtight. One source says the issue depends on storage and that, generally, there is not much to worry about if stored properly. [2] [1]

[3] Is treated bamboo, kosher for schach please? (Treated to prevent ruin by water.)

miyodeya · Oliver

The issue with materials that are "treated to withstand rain" would be if gezeirat tikra (no differentiation from a roof; see Sukkah 14a) applies to it. Some are of the opinion that only material that are structurally similar to a roof (e.g. wide planks of wood) are subsumed in this gezeirah (see responsa Shaarei Yosher 1:28 and works cited there). As indicated in the question, there are different opinions and so one should consult their LOR.

[7] Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim 629:6-7

Sefaria

he: במחצלת של קנים וקש ושיפה וגמי בין שהיא חלקה שהיא ראויה לשכיבה בין שאינה חלקה שאינה ראויה לשכיבה אם היא קטנה סתמא עומדת לשכיבה ומקבלת טומאה ואין מסככין בה אלא אם כן עשאה לסיכוך: הגה דהיינו שרוב בני אותה העיר עושין אותה לסיכוך (הרא"ש פ"ק דסוכה) ואם היא גדולה סתמא עומדת לסיכוך ומסככין בה אלא א"כ עשאה לשכיבה (דהיינו שמנהג המקום לשכב עליה) והני מילי שאין לה שפה אבל אם יש לה שפה בענין שראויה לקבל אפי' אם ניטל שפתה אין מסככין בה: הגה במקום שנהגו לקבוע מחצלאות בגגין כעין תקרה אין מסככין בהם (כל בו): יש להסתפק אם מותר להניח סולם על הגג כדי לסכך על גביו: הגה ולכן אין לסכך עליו ואפי' להניחו על הסכך להחזיקו אסור וה"ה בכל כלי המקבל טומאה כגון ספסל וכסא שמקבלין טומאת מדרס (מהרי"ל): en: Regarding a mat made of reeds, straw, papyrus, or bulrushes, whether or not the mat is smooth and thus fit to lie on or not smooth and not fit to lie on, if it is small, it is presumably intended to be lied on and is thus susceptible to ritual impurity and may not be used as schach, unless it was made to be schach. Rema: That is, the majority of the city residents make them for schach (Ros"h chapter 1 of Sukkah). If it is large, it is presumably intended to be schach and may be used for schach, unless it was made for lying on (meaning that the local practice is to lay on it). This is so if it does not have a rim. But if it has a rim and could thus serve as a receptacle, even if the rim is removed it may not be used as schach. RAMA: In a place where it is customary to affix a mat to a roof like a kind of ceiling, it may not be used for schach (Kol Bo). It is questionable whether it is permitted to lay a ladder on the roof so that the schach can be placed upon it. RAMA: Therefore, we do not put schach on it. Even laying it on the schach in order to hold it in place is forbidden. This is the law for any object that is susceptible to ritual impurity, such as a bench and a chair that receive midras uncleanness (Maharil).
